まで marks the endpoint or outer limit of a range or extent. In ordinary
range expressions, that endpoint is normally understood as part of the
range: 駅まで歩く "walk to the station", 5時まで働く.

The same particle also marks the far end of a range that surprises you
(子供まで知っている "even the kids").

The deadline cousin までに is a separate point.

@@ tldr

まで marks the endpoint or outer limit of a range or extent — normally
understood as included, as in 駅まで歩く. The same particle also marks the
far end of a range that surprises you (子供まで知っている "even the kids").

@@ extent

**Form:** `N + まで` · `V-dict + まで`

- **The limit is inside the stretch** — 5時まで働く is work through five
  o'clock, and 駅まで歩く gets you to the station. Whatever まで names is
  reached, not merely approached.
- **A clause before まで names the endpoint event, which needn't "last"** —
  彼が来るまで待つ "wait until he comes" uses a punctual event (来る) to mark
  the endpoint. What continues up to that point is the MAIN clause — 待つ —
  not the clause naming when it ends.
- **"To the point of"** — 声が出なくなるまで歌った "sang until my voice gave
  out" uses the same pattern: the endpoint can be temporal, resultant or
  scalar depending on the sentence, not only a place.
- **Stacked particles narrow it** — までは sets the stretch against what comes
  after it (ここまでは分かる "I follow it this far"). The base reading doesn't
  change; the added particle does its usual work.

!!! warning "Pitfall"
    With quantities, まで can mark an upper limit rather than an estimate:
    10人まで means up to ten and no more. For "about ten people" you want
    10人ぐらい, and the two are easy to swap when the English is loose.

@@ even

**Form:** `N + まで`

- **Surprise at how far the range got** — the main point is the
  unexpectedly broad extent: the news reached even children. Which member
  marks that surprising edge can itself carry social weight, not just
  illustrate the range.
- **It is the extent reading, on a social scale** — the range runs from the
  obvious cases out to this one, and まで marks where it stopped. Same
  particle, same idea.
- **さえ overlaps with まで in its scalar "even" use** — 子供さえ知っている is
  itself an ordinary "even children know" reading. The two aren't cleanly
  split by meaning here; which one a speaker reaches for often comes down to
  register or emphasis.
- **さえ has a second job まで doesn't share** — in Xさえ〜ば, さえ marks X as
  a sufficient condition: ハルさえ来れば大丈夫 "as long as Haru comes, it'll be
  fine". まで doesn't do this conditional work.
- **It carries feeling, and that can misfire** — the surprise in まで about a
  person reads as warmth or as dismay depending on who is being included, so
  まで about a person can land as "even them".
