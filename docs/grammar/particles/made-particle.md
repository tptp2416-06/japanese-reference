# まで — Limit, Extent, and Even

## <ruby>駅<rt>えき</rt></ruby>まで<ruby>歩<rt>ある</rt></ruby>いた。 / <ruby>彼<rt>かれ</rt></ruby>まで<ruby>知<rt>し</rt></ruby>っている。

---

## JLPT Level {#jlpt-level}

!!! abstract "JLPT Level — N5 Foundation / N4 Core / N3 Extended"
    Spatial and temporal limits (<ruby>駅<rt>えき</rt></ruby>まで) are N5. The deadline distinction (までに) and basic emphatic inclusion (<ruby>彼<rt>かれ</rt></ruby>まで) are N4. True mastery — the calibration between まで and さえ, the extent/result constructions (<ruby>泣<rt>な</rt></ruby>くまで<ruby>待<rt>ま</rt></ruby>つ), the concessive までして, and the nuanced difference between inclusive "even" and limit "up to" — pushes into high N3. This page maps the full particle landscape.

    | Tier | Pattern | Description | Example |
    |------|---------|-------------|---------|
    | N5 — Foundation | まで (spatial limit) | Up to a place | <ruby>駅<rt>えき</rt></ruby>まで<ruby>行<rt>い</rt></ruby>く。 |
    | N5 — Foundation | まで (temporal limit) | Until a time | 3<ruby>時<rt>じ</rt></ruby>まで<ruby>働<rt>はたら</rt></ruby>く。 |
    | **N4 — Core ← You are here** | **までに (deadline)** | **By a specific point** | **<ruby>月曜日<rt>げつようび</rt></ruby>までに<ruby>出<rt>だ</rt></ruby>す。** |
    | N4 — Core | **まで (inclusive "even")** | **Emphatic inclusion — "even X"** | **<ruby>彼<rt>かれ</rt></ruby>まで<ruby>知<rt>し</rt></ruby>っている。** |
    | **N3 — Extended** | **まで (extent/result)** | **To the point of / so much that** | **<ruby>泣<rt>な</rt></ruby>くまで<ruby>言<rt>い</rt></ruby>われた。** |
    | **N3 — Extended** | **までして** | **Going so far as to / even doing** | **<ruby>金<rt>かね</rt></ruby>を<ruby>借<rt>か</rt></ruby>りるまでして<ruby>買<rt>か</rt></ruby>った。** |
    | N3 — Extended | **までもない** | **Not even necessary to** | **<ruby>言<rt>い</rt></ruby>うまでもない。** |
    | N2 — Awareness | までのことだ | Merely a matter of / only | <ruby>練習<rt>れんしゅう</rt></ruby>までのことだ。 |
    | N2 — Awareness | までだ / までの話 | That's all there is to it | <ruby>謝<rt>あやま</rt></ruby>るまでだ。 |

## Quick Summary {#quick-summary}

!!! tip "At a glance"
    - **まで** is fundamentally a **limit marker**. It draws a line in space, time, or logic and says: "up to this point, and no further." <ruby>駅<rt>えき</rt></ruby>まで = up to the station. 3<ruby>時<rt>じ</rt></ruby>まで = until 3:00.
    - **までに** is the **deadline particle**. It marks the point by which an action must be completed. <ruby>月曜日<rt>げつようび</rt></ruby>までに = by Monday. The action finishes **before or at** the line; it does not continue **up to** the line.
    - **まで as "even"** is the N3 leap. When attached to surprising or unexpected nouns, it means "to the extent that even X" or "all the way to X." <ruby>彼<rt>かれ</rt></ruby>まで<ruby>知<rt>し</rt></ruby>っている = "Even he knows." The limit marker has become an **inclusion marker**.
    - **Extent/result まで** pushes further: it marks the **degree or consequence** of an action. <ruby>待<rt>ま</rt></ruby>つまでもない = "not to the extent of waiting" = "no need to wait." <ruby>泣<rt>な</rt></ruby>くまで<ruby>言<rt>い</rt></ruby>われた = "I was told [something] to the point of crying."
    - **までして** attaches to verb stems and means "going so far as to [extreme action]." It is the grammar of **desperation, dedication, or shock at someone's lengths**.
    - The critical calibration: **まで includes the endpoint**; **までに points at the endpoint** as a deadline. You walk **to** the station (まで). You submit **by** Monday (までに).

## Core Meaning {#core-meaning}

!!! note "Core Rule"
    Use まで when you want to mark an **endpoint, limit, or surprising inclusion**. The particle treats the preceding noun as the farthest reach of a range — whether spatial, temporal, social, or emotional.

| <ruby>駅<rt>えき</rt></ruby>まで<ruby>歩<rt>ある</rt></ruby>いた。 | I walked to the station [spatial limit]. |
|---|---|
| | *Context: The station is the endpoint of the walk. The particle includes the station as the destination.* |

| 3<ruby>時<rt>じ</rt></ruby>まで<ruby>勉強<rt>べんきょう</rt></ruby>する。 | I will study until 3:00 [temporal limit]. |
|---|---|
| | *Context: 3:00 is the endpoint of the study session. The studying continues up to and includes the moment of 3:00.* |

| <ruby>月曜日<rt>げつようび</rt></ruby>までにレポートを<ruby>出<rt>だ</rt></ruby>す。 | I will submit the report by Monday [deadline]. |
|---|---|
| | *Context: Monday is the latest acceptable moment. The submission happens **at or before** Monday, not continuously **up to** Monday.* |

| <ruby>彼<rt>かれ</rt></ruby>まで<ruby>知<rt>し</rt></ruby>っている。 | Even he knows [emphatic inclusion]. |
|---|---|
| | *Context: He is the surprising endpoint of the "people who know" range. The particle includes him as unexpectedly far along the knowledge spectrum.* |

| <ruby>金<rt>かね</rt></ruby>を<ruby>借<rt>か</rt></ruby>りるまでして<ruby>買<rt>か</rt></ruby>った。 | I bought it going so far as to borrow money [extreme action]. |
|---|---|
| | *Context: Borrowing money is the extreme endpoint of measures taken. The grammar expresses shock at the lengths gone to.* |

## The Five Functions of まで {#the-five-functions-of-made}

### 1. Spatial Limit — "Up to / To"

The original, physical meaning. まで marks the destination or farthest point reached by movement.

| <ruby>家<rt>いえ</rt></ruby>まで<ruby>送<rt>おく</rt></ruby>る。 | I'll walk you home [to your house]. |
|---|---|
| | *Context: The house is the limit of the escort. The particle includes the destination.* |

| <ruby>日本<rt>にほん</rt></ruby>まで<ruby>来<rt>き</rt></ruby>た。 | I came all the way to Japan [distance]. |
|---|---|
| | *Context: Japan is the far endpoint of the journey. The grammar can imply effort or surprise at the distance.* |

| どこまで<ruby>行<rt>い</rt></ruby>くの？ | How far are you going? [questioning limit]. |
|---|---|
| | *Context: Asking about the spatial endpoint. Can be literal or metaphorical ("how far will you take this?").* |

!!! note "まで includes the endpoint"
    <ruby>駅<rt>えき</rt></ruby>まで<ruby>行<rt>い</rt></ruby>く means you arrive at the station. If you want to say "toward the station but not necessarily arriving," use <ruby>駅<rt>えき</rt></ruby>の<ruby>方<rt>ほう</rt></ruby>へ or <ruby>駅<rt>えき</rt></ruby>に<ruby>向<rt>む</rt></ruby>かって.

### 2. Temporal Limit — "Until"

まで marks the time at which an action or state ends. The action continues **up to and includes** the time marker.

| <ruby>夜<rt>よる</rt></ruby>まで<ruby>働<rt>はたら</rt></ruby>く。 | I work until night [temporal endpoint]. |
|---|---|
| | *Context: Work continues through the day and stops at nightfall.* |

| <ruby>来週<rt>らいしゅう</rt></ruby>まで<ruby>待<rt>ま</rt></ruby>ってください。 | Please wait until next week [request for duration]. |
|---|---|
| | *Context: The waiting period extends up to next week. The endpoint is included.* |

| <ruby>死<rt>し</rt></ruby>ぬまで<ruby>愛<rt>あい</rt></ruby>す。 | I will love you until death [romantic extreme]. |
|---|---|
| | *Context: Death is the absolute temporal limit. The grammar is poetic and absolute.* |

!!! warning "Do not confuse まで with から"
    3<ruby>時<rt>じ</rt></ruby>まで<ruby>働<rt>はたら</rt></ruby>く = work until 3:00 (endpoint). 3<ruby>時<rt>じ</rt></ruby>から<ruby>働<rt>はたら</rt></ruby>く = work from 3:00 (starting point). These are opposite directions.

### 3. Deadline — までに "By"

This is the N4 distinction that separates functional Japanese from broken Japanese. までに marks a **completion deadline**, not a duration endpoint.

| <ruby>月曜日<rt>げつようび</rt></ruby>までに<ruby>出<rt>だ</rt></ruby>す。 | Submit by Monday [deadline]. |
|---|---|
| | *Context: The submission must be completed at or before Monday. The action is punctual, not durative.* |

| 3<ruby>時<rt>じ</rt></ruby>までに<ruby>終<rt>お</rt></ruby>わらせる。 | Finish by 3:00 [deadline]. |
|---|---|
| | *Context: Completion target. The finishing happens before the clock strikes 3:00.* |

| <ruby>来週<rt>らいしゅう</rt></ruby>までに<ruby>連絡<rt>れんらく</rt></ruby>する。 | I will contact you by next week [deadline]. |
|---|---|
| | *Context: The contact happens at some point before next week ends. It is not continuous contact up to next week.* |

!!! note "The までに vs. まで distinction"
    | <ruby>月曜日<rt>げつようび</rt></ruby>までレポートを<ruby>書<rt>か</rt></ruby>く。 | I write the report until Monday [duration]. |
    | <ruby>月曜日<rt>げつようび</rt></ruby>までにレポートを<ruby>出<rt>だ</rt></ruby>す。 | I submit the report by Monday [deadline]. |

    The first means the writing continues through Monday. The second means the submission must happen before Monday passes. This is the most consequential N4 distinction for まで.

### 4. Emphatic Inclusion — "Even"

At N3, まで detaches from physical limits and attaches to **social or logical extremes**. It means "to the extent that even X" — implying that X is the surprising far end of a range.

| <ruby>子供<rt>こども</rt></ruby>まで<ruby>知<rt>し</rt></ruby>っている。 | Even children know [surprising inclusion]. |
|---|---|
| | *Context: Children are the unexpected endpoint of knowledge. The grammar implies "if even children know, everyone knows."* |

| <ruby>彼<rt>かれ</rt></ruby>まで<ruby>来<rt>き</rt></ruby>た。 | Even he came [surprising attendance]. |
|---|---|
| | *Context: He was the least likely to attend. His presence marks the extreme of the group.* |

| 100<ruby>円<rt>えん</rt></ruby>まで<ruby>出<rt>だ</rt></ruby>せない。 | I can't pay even 100 yen [extreme smallness]. |
|---|---|
| | *Context: 100 yen is tiny, yet the speaker cannot manage even that. The grammar makes the poverty feel absolute.* |

| <ruby>先生<rt>せんせい</rt></ruby>まで<ruby>間違<rt>まちが</rt></ruby>えた。 | Even the teacher made a mistake [surprising fallibility]. |
|---|---|
| | *Context: The teacher is the authority figure — the last person expected to err. The grammar frames the mistake as reaching the highest level.* |

!!! note "まで "even" vs. さえ "even"**
    Both mean "even." さえ is colder and more analytical: <ruby>子供<rt>こども</rt></ruby>さえ<ruby>知<rt>し</rt></ruby>っている = "even children know [and that's the baseline]." まで is warmer and more emotional: <ruby>子供<rt>こども</rt></ruby>まで<ruby>知<rt>し</rt></ruby>っている = "even children know [can you believe it?]." さえ focuses on the **minimum sufficient condition**; まで focuses on the **surprising extent**.

    - <ruby>子供<rt>こども</rt></ruby>さえできる = "Even a child can do it [so it's easy]."
    - <ruby>子供<rt>こども</rt></ruby>までできる = "Even children can do it [wow, it's spread everywhere]."

### 5. Extent & Degree — "To the Point of"

At high N3, まで marks the **result or consequence** of an action — the degree to which something goes.

| <ruby>泣<rt>な</rt></ruby>くまで<ruby>言<rt>い</rt></ruby>われた。 | I was told [it] to the point of crying [emotional extent]. |
|---|---|
| | *Context: The telling was so harsh or persistent that crying resulted. まで marks the consequence as the endpoint.* |

| <ruby>待<rt>ま</rt></ruby>つまでもない。 | Not to the extent of waiting = No need to wait [negative extent]. |
|---|---|
| | *Context: The までもない pattern means "not even necessary to [do]." The extent is so small that the action is unwarranted.* |

| <ruby>驚<rt>おどろ</rt></ruby>くまでもない。 | Hardly surprising / Nothing to be shocked about [negative extent]. |
|---|---|
| | *Context: The event is so expected that being surprised would be excessive.* |

| <ruby>知<rt>し</rt></ruby>るまでもない。 | There's no need to even know [negative extent]. |
|---|---|
| | *Context: The information is so trivial that knowing it is unnecessary.* |

## までして — Going So Far As To {#made-shite}

This is the N3 pattern of **extreme measures**. It attaches to the stem of a verb and means "to the extent of doing [extreme action]."

| <ruby>金<rt>かね</rt></ruby>を<ruby>借<rt>か</rt></ruby>りるまでして<ruby>買<rt>か</rt></ruby>った。 | I bought it going so far as to borrow money [desperation]. |
|---|---|
| | *Context: Borrowing money is the extreme measure. The grammar expresses that the purchase required crossing a serious line.* |

| <ruby>嘘<rt>うそ</rt></ruby>をつくまでして<ruby>逃<rt>に</rt></ruby>げた。 | He ran away going so far as to lie [moral extreme]. |
|---|---|
| | *Context: Lying is the transgressive act. までして frames it as the shocking length gone to.* |

| <ruby>徹夜<rt>てつや</rt></ruby>するまでして<ruby>勉強<rt>べんきょう</rt></ruby>した。 | I studied to the point of staying up all night [dedication]. |
|---|---|
| | *Context: Can be positive (dedication) or negative (unhealthy obsession). Context determines the valence.* |

| <ruby>会社<rt>かいしゃ</rt></ruby>を<ruby>辞<rt>や</rt></ruby>めるまでして<ruby>結婚<rt>けっこん</rt></ruby>した。 | I got married going so far as to quit my job [life-altering extreme]. |
|---|---|
| | *Context: The sacrifice is framed as massive. までして makes the listener feel the weight of the choice.* |

!!! note "までして vs. てまで"
    <ruby>金<rt>かね</rt></ruby>を<ruby>借<rt>か</rt></ruby>りてまで<ruby>買<rt>か</rt></ruby>った is a related N3 pattern with slightly different nuance. てまで focuses on the **means** (borrowing money as the method). までして focuses on the **extreme lengths** (the shock that borrowing was necessary). Both are acceptable, but までして sounds more dramatic.

!!! note "までして is often critical or amazed"
    When describing others, までして often carries judgment: <ruby>彼<rt>かれ</rt></ruby>は<ruby>金<rt>かね</rt></ruby>を<ruby>借<rt>か</rt></ruby>りるまでしてギャンブルした = "He gambled to the extent of borrowing money [how foolish]." When describing oneself, it can carry pride or regret.

## までもない — Not Even Necessary {#made-mo-nai}

This N3 pattern attaches to the dictionary form of a verb and means "not to the extent of [doing]" = "there is no need to [do]."

| <ruby>言<rt>い</rt></ruby>うまでもない。 | It goes without saying [most common]. |
|---|---|
| | *Context: The most lexicalized までもない sentence. It means the point is so obvious that stating it is unnecessary.* |

| <ruby>心配<rt>しんぱい</rt></ruby>するまでもない。 | There's no need to worry [reassurance]. |
|---|---|
| | *Context: The situation is not severe enough to warrant worry.* |

| <ruby>驚<rt>おどろ</rt></ruby>くまでもない。 | Hardly surprising [dismissive]. |
|---|---|
| | *Context: The event is so expected that shock would be excessive.* |

| <ruby>説明<rt>せつめい</rt></ruby>するまでもない。 | No explanation is necessary [confidence]. |
|---|---|
| | *Context: The speaker considers the matter self-evident.* |

!!! note "までもない as understatement"
    Japanese often uses までもない to be polite. <ruby>心配<rt>しんぱい</rt></ruby>するまでもない sounds gentler than <ruby>心配<rt>しんぱい</rt></ruby>しないでください because it frames the worry as unnecessary rather than forbidding it.

## The Critical Distinctions {#the-critical-distinctions}

### まで vs. までに

| | まで | までに |
|---|---|---|
| **Core logic** | Limit of duration / endpoint of movement | Deadline for completion |
| **Action type** | Durative (continues up to the line) | Punctual (finishes at or before the line) |
| **Includes endpoint?** | Yes | No — the endpoint is the latest possible moment |
| **English** | until / to / up to | by |
| **Example** | 3<ruby>時<rt>じ</rt></ruby>まで<ruby>働<rt>はたら</rt></ruby>く (work until 3:00) | 3<ruby>時<rt>じ</rt></ruby>までに<ruby>終<rt>お</rt></ruby>わる (finish by 3:00) |
| **Example** | <ruby>月曜日<rt>げつようび</rt></ruby>まで<ruby>待<rt>ま</rt></ruby>つ (wait until Monday) | <ruby>月曜日<rt>げつようび</rt></ruby>までに<ruby>出<rt>だ</rt></ruby>す (submit by Monday) |

!!! failure "Deadline error"
    <ruby>月曜日<rt>げつようび</rt></ruby>まで<ruby>出<rt>だ</rt></ruby>す。

    ~This means "I submit [continuously] until Monday." It implies the submission process lasts all week. For a deadline, までに is mandatory.~

!!! success "Correct"
    <ruby>月曜日<rt>げつようび</rt></ruby>までに<ruby>出<rt>だ</rt></ruby>す。

### まで vs. さえ (Inclusive "Even")

| | まで "even" | さえ "even" |
|---|---|---|
| **Emotional tone** | Surprised, warm, emotional | Cold, analytical, minimal |
| **Logic** | "To the extent that even X" | "X is the minimum sufficient condition" |
| **Direction** | Looks up at surprising inclusion | Looks down at baseline inclusion |
| **Example** | <ruby>子供<rt>こども</rt></ruby>まで<ruby>知<rt>し</rt></ruby>っている (wow, even kids know) | <ruby>子供<rt>こども</rt></ruby>さえ<ruby>知<rt>し</rt></ruby>っている (even kids know, so it's easy) |
| **Example** | <ruby>彼<rt>かれ</rt></ruby>まで<ruby>来<rt>き</rt></ruby>た (even he came — unexpected) | <ruby>彼<rt>かれ</rt></ruby>さえ<ruby>来<rt>く</rt></ruby>れば<ruby>大丈夫<rt>だいじょうぶ</rt></ruby> (if even he comes, we're fine) |
| **Social risk** | Can sound dramatic | Can sound dismissive |

!!! note "The two "evens" in one sentence"
    <ruby>子供<rt>こども</rt></ruby>まで<ruby>知<rt>し</rt></ruby>っている = Even children know [surprise at how widespread].
    <ruby>子供<rt>こども</rt></ruby>さえ<ruby>知<rt>し</rt></ruby>っている = Even children know [proof of how simple].

    The first is about the **range** of knowers. The second is about the **difficulty** of the knowledge.

### まで vs. までで

| | まで | までで |
|---|---|---|
| **Core meaning** | Up to and including | Up to and no further / only |
| **Implication** | The endpoint is reached | The endpoint is reached and that's all |
| **Example** | <ruby>駅<rt>えき</rt></ruby>まで<ruby>行<rt>い</rt></ruby>く (go to the station) | <ruby>駅<rt>えき</rt></ruby>までで<ruby>行<rt>い</rt></ruby>く (only go to the station [and stop]) |
| **Emotional nuance** | Neutral | Limiting, sometimes disappointed |

!!! note "までで as "only"**
    <ruby>駅<rt>えき</rt></ruby>までで<ruby>帰<rt>かえ</rt></ruby>った = "I only went to the station and came back." The で adds a sense of "that was the extent of it, nothing more." This is N3 nuance.

## Grammar Analysis {#grammar-analysis}

### Function in Context

- <ruby>駅<rt>えき</rt></ruby>まで<ruby>歩<rt>ある</rt></ruby>いた: 駅 is the spatial limit. まで marks the destination. The particle includes the station as the reached endpoint.
- 3<ruby>時<rt>じ</rt></ruby>まで<ruby>働<rt>はたら</rt></ruby>く: 3時 is the temporal limit. まで marks the end of the work period. The particle includes 3:00 as the final moment of work.
- <ruby>月曜日<rt>げつようび</rt></ruby>までに<ruby>出<rt>だ</rt></ruby>す: 月曜日 is the deadline. までに marks the latest acceptable moment. The particle points at Monday as a boundary to be met, not a duration to be filled.
- <ruby>彼<rt>かれ</rt></ruby>まで<ruby>知<rt>し</rt></ruby>っている: 彼 is the surprising inclusion. まで marks him as the far endpoint of the knowledge range. The grammar implies "the knowledge has spread so far that even he possesses it."
- <ruby>金<rt>かね</rt></ruby>を<ruby>借<rt>か</rt></ruby>りるまでして<ruby>買<rt>か</rt></ruby>った: 借りる is the extreme measure. までして marks the action as the shocking limit of what was done. The grammar frames the purchase as requiring transgressive lengths.
- <ruby>言<rt>い</rt></ruby>うまでもない: 言う is the unnecessary action. までもない negates the extent, saying "not to the degree of speaking." The grammar treats silence as the appropriate response.

### Construction {#construction}

#### Basic Limit: Noun + まで

| Type | Example | Meaning |
|------|---------|---------|
| Spatial | <ruby>駅<rt>えき</rt></ruby>まで | to the station |
| Temporal | 3<ruby>時<rt>じ</rt></ruby>まで | until 3:00 |
| Abstract | ここまで | up to this point |
| Abstract | その<ruby>程度<rt>ていど</rt></ruby>まで | to that extent |

#### Deadline: Noun + までに

| Type | Example | Meaning |
|------|---------|---------|
| Temporal | <ruby>月曜日<rt>げつようび</rt></ruby>までに | by Monday |
| Temporal | <ruby>来週<rt>らいしゅう</rt></ruby>までに | by next week |
| Temporal | 3<ruby>時<rt>じ</rt></ruby>までに | by 3:00 |
| Abstract | <ruby>試験<rt>しけん</rt></ruby>までに | by the exam |

#### Emphatic Inclusion: Noun + まで

| Type | Example | Meaning |
|------|---------|---------|
| Person | <ruby>彼<rt>かれ</rt></ruby>まで | even him |
| Person | <ruby>子供<rt>こども</rt></ruby>まで | even children |
| Amount | 100<ruby>円<rt>えん</rt></ruby>まで | even 100 yen |
| Status | <ruby>先生<rt>せんせい</rt></ruby>まで | even the teacher |

#### Extent/Result: Verb + まで

| Type | Pattern | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| Plain verb | V + まで | <ruby>知<rt>し</rt></ruby>るまで |
| Negative extent | V + までもない | <ruby>言<rt>い</rt></ruby>うまでもない |
| Extreme measure | V-stem + までして | <ruby>借<rt>か</rt></ruby>りるまでして |

#### まで + Particle Stacking

| Pattern | Example | Meaning |
|---------|---------|---------|
| まで + が | <ruby>彼<rt>かれ</rt></ruby>までが | even he [subject focus] |
| まで + を | それまでを | up to that point [object focus] |
| まで + の | 3<ruby>時<rt>じ</rt></ruby>までの | until 3:00 [attributive] |
| まで + で | <ruby>駅<rt>えき</rt></ruby>までで | only to the station [limit] |

!!! warning "Do not use までに with durative verbs"
    !!! failure "Aspect error"
        <ruby>月曜日<rt>げつようび</rt></ruby>までに<ruby>待<rt>ま</rt></ruby>つ。
    !!! success "Correct"
        <ruby>月曜日<rt>げつようび</rt></ruby>まで<ruby>待<rt>ま</rt></ruby>つ。 (Wait until Monday.)
        <ruby>月曜日<rt>げつようび</rt></ruby>までに<ruby>終<rt>お</rt></ruby>わらせる。 (Finish by Monday.)

    ~待つ is durative. It wants a duration endpoint (まで), not a deadline (までに). までに requires a punctual/completive verb.~

## Japanese Mindset {#japanese-mindset}

The particle まで reveals a cultural preference for **framing boundaries as inclusive rather than exclusive**.

- **Inclusive endpoints.** <ruby>駅<rt>えき</rt></ruby>まで<ruby>行<rt>い</rt></ruby>く includes the station. English "up to the station" can feel ambiguous about arrival; Japanese まで is clear: you got there. This reflects a broader tendency to treat limits as reached rather than approached.
- **Deadline culture.** The までに distinction is essential in Japanese workplaces because it encodes **punctuality as moral obligation**. Missing a までに deadline is not merely late — it is a breach of social contract.
- **Emotional "even" as social thermometer.** <ruby>彼<rt>かれ</rt></ruby>まで<ruby>来<rt>き</rt></ruby>た does not merely report attendance. It says "the social temperature of this event is so high that it reached even him." The grammar measures collective enthusiasm.
- **までして as moral boundary marker.** When Japanese speakers use までして, they are often commenting on whether someone crossed a moral line. <ruby>嘘<rt>うそ</rt></ruby>をつくまでして expresses that lying was the transgressive limit — the point where "too far" was reached.
- **までもない as humble deflection.** <ruby>言<rt>い</rt></ruby>うまでもない is a polite way to say "obviously" without sounding arrogant. It frames the obviousness as so extreme that mentioning it would be redundant.

## Nuance Comparison {#nuance-comparison}

| Grammar Point | Core Meaning | Endpoint Logic | Emotional Register | JLPT |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| まで (spatial/temporal) | Up to / until | Included | Neutral | N5 |
| までに | By | Pointed at, not included | Neutral, urgent | N4 |
| まで (even) | Even / to the extent that | Surprising inclusion | Surprised, warm | N3 |
| さえ | Even / if only | Minimum sufficient | Cold, analytical | N3 |
| までして | To the extent of doing | Extreme measure | Shocked, critical, admiring | N3 |
| までもない | Not even necessary | Negated extent | Understated, dismissive | N3 |
| までで | Only up to / and that's it | Limited | Disappointed, restricting | N3 |
| から | From | Starting point | Neutral | N5 |
| までのことだ | Merely a matter of | Downplayed extent | Humble | N2 |

**まで vs. から:** から marks the starting point; まで marks the endpoint. They often pair: 朝から夜まで = from morning until night.

**まで vs. までに:** This is the most tested N4 distinction. まで = duration/endpoint. までに = deadline. 3時まで働く (work until 3). 3時までに終わる (finish by 3).

**まで "even" vs. さえ:** Both translate to "even." まで is emotional and surprised. さえ is logical and conditional. 子供まで知っている = "even children know [amazing]." 子供さえ知っていれば = "if even children know [then it's sufficient]."

**までして vs. てまで:** Both mark extremes. 金を借りるまでして買った = dramatic, narrative. 金を借りてまで買った = means-focused, slightly more detached. までして is more common in spoken emotional storytelling.

## Register and Formality {#register-and-formality}

=== ":material-chat-outline: Casual"
    <ruby>彼<rt>かれ</rt></ruby>まで<ruby>知<rt>し</rt></ruby>ってたの？

    *"Even he knew?"*

    **Shift:** 知ってた is casual past of 知っている. の is casual question marker. まで carries surprise in everyday speech.

=== ":material-chat-outline: Casual (extent)"
    <ruby>泣<rt>な</rt></ruby>くまで<ruby>言<rt>い</rt></ruby>われたんだよ。

    *"I got told [it] to the point of crying, you know."*

    **Shift:** んだよ explanatory + assertive. まで marks emotional extent. Standard complaint register.

=== ":material-tie: Basic Polite"
    <ruby>月曜日<rt>げつようび</rt></ruby>までにご<ruby>提出<rt>ていしゅつ</rt></ruby>ください。

    *"Please submit by Monday."*

    **Shift:** までに is mandatory for deadlines. ご提出 is polite nominal. This is office-standard.

=== ":material-tie: Basic Polite (inclusive)"
    <ruby>先生<rt>せんせい</rt></ruby>まで<ruby>間違<rt>まちが</rt></ruby>えられました。

    *"Even the teacher made a mistake."*

    **Shift:** られました is polite passive. まで marks the surprising inclusion with formal grammar.

=== ":material-briefcase-outline: Extra Polite"
    <ruby>来週<rt>らいしゅう</rt></ruby><ruby>月曜日<rt>げつようび</rt></ruby>までに、<ruby>必<rt>かなら</rt></ruby>ずお<ruby>送<rt>おく</rt></ruby>りいたします。

    *"I will certainly send it by next Monday."*

    **Shift:** 必ず adds commitment. お送りいたします is humble. までに is the deadline anchor.

=== ":material-briefcase-outline: Extra Polite (set phrase)"
    <ruby>言<rt>い</rt></ruby>うまでもございません。

    *"It goes without saying."*

    **Shift:** ございません is polite negative of ある. までもない in keigo form. Ultra-formal reassurance.

## Common Mistakes {#common-mistakes}

### 1. Using まで instead of までに for deadlines

!!! failure "Deadline error"
    <ruby>月曜日<rt>げつようび</rt></ruby>まで<ruby>出<rt>だ</rt></ruby>す。

    ~This means "I submit continuously until Monday" — the submission process lasts all week. For a deadline, までに is mandatory.~

!!! success "Correct"
    <ruby>月曜日<rt>げつようび</rt></ruby>までに<ruby>出<rt>だ</rt></ruby>す。

### 2. Using までに with durative verbs

!!! failure "Aspect error"
    3<ruby>時<rt>じ</rt></ruby>までに<ruby>待<rt>ま</rt></ruby>つ。

    ~待つ is durative. It wants a duration endpoint (まで), not a deadline (までに). までに requires a completive action.~

!!! success "Correct"
    3<ruby>時<rt>じ</rt></ruby>まで<ruby>待<rt>ま</rt></ruby>つ。 (Wait until 3:00.)
    3<ruby>時<rt>じ</rt></ruby>までに<ruby>終<rt>お</rt></ruby>わる。 (Finish by 3:00.)

### 3. Confusing まで "even" with さえ "even"

!!! failure "Pragmatic error"
    <ruby>子供<rt>こども</rt></ruby>まで<ruby>知<rt>し</rt></ruby>っていれば、<ruby>大丈夫<rt>だいじょうぶ</rt></ruby>だ。 (when meaning "if even children know, it's easy")

    ~This is grammatically possible but pragmatically odd. まで "even" is surprised at the range. さえ "even" is conditional — it establishes the minimum sufficient condition. For "if even X, then Y" logic, さえ is standard.~

!!! success "Correct"
    <ruby>子供<rt>こども</rt></ruby>さえ<ruby>知<rt>し</rt></ruby>っていれば、<ruby>大丈夫<rt>だいじょうぶ</rt></ruby>だ。

### 4. Using まで for "about" or "approximately"

!!! failure "Semantic error"
    100<ruby>人<rt>にん</rt></ruby>まで<ruby>来<rt>き</rt></ruby>た。 (meaning "about 100 people came")

    ~まで means "up to 100" = "100 or fewer." For approximation, use ぐらい or ほど: 100人ぐらい来た.~

!!! success "Correct"
    100<ruby>人<rt>にん</rt></ruby>まで<ruby>来<rt>き</rt></ruby>た。 (Up to 100 people came [maximum].)
    100<ruby>人<rt>にん</rt></ruby>ぐらい<ruby>来<rt>き</rt></ruby>た。 (About 100 people came.)

### 5. Translating English "by" as まで without に

!!! failure "Transfer error"
    <ruby>明日<rt>あした</rt></ruby>まで<ruby>終<rt>お</rt></ruby>わる。

    ~This means "I finish until tomorrow" — the finishing continues up to tomorrow. For "finish by tomorrow," までに is required.~

!!! success "Correct"
    <ruby>明日<rt>あした</rt></ruby>までに<ruby>終<rt>お</rt></ruby>わる。

### 6. Using までもない with positive verbs

!!! failure "Polarity error"
    <ruby>言<rt>い</rt></ruby>うまでもある。

    ~までもない is a fixed negative pattern. There is no positive までもある.~

!!! success "Correct"
    <ruby>言<rt>い</rt></ruby>うまでもない。

## Hidden Points {#hidden-points}

- **どこまで is the grammar of escalation.** In arguments, どこまで<ruby>行<rt>い</rt></ruby>くの？ means "how far will you take this?" The spatial particle becomes a metaphor for interpersonal escalation.
- **まで can attach to entire clauses in casual speech:** <ruby>彼<rt>かれ</rt></ruby>が<ruby>来<rt>く</rt></ruby>るまで<ruby>知<rt>し</rt></ruby>らなかった = "I didn't know until he came." The clause 彼が来る is the temporal limit. This is standard but sometimes omitted from textbooks.
- **ここまで and そこまで are emotional thresholds.** ここまで<ruby>来<rt>き</rt></ruby>た = "we've come this far." そこまで<ruby>言<rt>い</rt></ruby>う？ = "will you go that far [in your words]?" The demonstrative + まで measures emotional distance.
- **までのことだ downplays effort.** <ruby>練習<rt>れんしゅう</rt></ruby>までのことだ = "it's merely a matter of practice." The までのこと frames the obstacle as limited and surmountable. This is N2 but appears in N3 reading.
- **までだ as resignation:** <ruby>謝<rt>あやま</rt></ruby>るまでだ = "all I can do is apologize." The grammar limits the speaker's options to one endpoint. This is N2 but conceptually related.
- **までに can stack with まで for range:** <ruby>月曜日<rt>げつようび</rt></ruby>から<ruby>金曜日<rt>きんようび</rt></ruby>までに = "at some point between Monday and Friday." The から…までに frame creates a window.
- **まで "even" with money often implies poverty.** 100<ruby>円<rt>えん</rt></ruby>まで<ruby>出<rt>だ</rt></ruby>せない focuses on the smallness. 100<ruby>万<rt>まん</rt></ruby><ruby>円<rt>えん</rt></ruby>まで<ruby>出<rt>だ</rt></ruby>せる focuses on the largeness. The direction of surprise depends on the number's relation to expectation.
- **までして is often used in gossip.** Because it marks extreme measures, it is perfect for narrating others' questionable choices: <ruby>離婚<rt>りこん</rt></ruby>するまでして<ruby>結婚<rt>けっこん</rt></ruby>した = "they got married going so far as to divorce [someone else]." The grammar does the moralizing.
- **<ruby>子供<rt>こども</rt></ruby>まで<ruby>知<rt>し</rt></ruby>っている is a set phrase for "common knowledge."** It implies "if even children know, it is universal." The grammar uses the surprising endpoint to prove the rule.
- **まで can be used for "even" with time:** <ruby>夜<rt>よる</rt></ruby>まで<ruby>待<rt>ま</rt></ruby>つ can mean "wait until night" (limit) or "wait even into the night" (surprising extent). Context and intonation disambiguate.

## Learner Stage {#learner-stage}

| Stage | What It Looks Like |
|-------|-------------------|
| **Now — N5** | Use まで for spatial and temporal limits correctly. Understand that まで includes the endpoint. Pair から with まで for ranges. |
| **Developing — N5→N4** | Master the までに deadline distinction. Use までに for completive actions, まで for durative actions. Begin using まで for basic emphatic inclusion (<ruby>彼<rt>かれ</rt></ruby>まで). |
| **Automatic — N4→N3** | Deploy まで "even" naturally for surprising inclusions. Use までして for extreme measures. Recognize までもない as understatement. Distinguish まで from さえ in emotional temperature. |
| **Mastery** | You control the full まで spectrum: spatial limits (<ruby>駅<rt>えき</rt></ruby>まで), temporal limits (3<ruby>時<rt>じ</rt></ruby>まで), deadlines (<ruby>月曜日<rt>げつようび</rt></ruby>までに), emphatic inclusion (<ruby>彼<rt>かれ</rt></ruby>まで), extent/result (<ruby>泣<rt>な</rt></ruby>くまで), extreme measures (<ruby>借<rt>か</rt></ruby>りるまでして), and negated necessity (<ruby>言<rt>い</rt></ruby>うまでもない). You never confuse まで with までに, and you calibrate まで against さえ with precision. |
